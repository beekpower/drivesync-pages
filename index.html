drivesync

A personal, self-hosted Google Drive sync tool.

drivesync keeps a folder on its owner's own server in two-way sync with their Google Drive. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are stored locally as OpenDocument files (.odt, .ods, .odp), and local edits are uploaded back into the original Google files.

It is a private tool for a single user. It is not offered as a service, and no one else's account is accessed.
